How to Check Sri Lanka ID Card Details Online

In Sri Lanka, every citizen over the age of 15 is issued a National Identity Card (NIC) by the Department for Registration of Persons (DRP). This number is not random: it systematically encodes vital demographic details according to official government standards.

Whether you hold an old 9-digit NIC (ending in V or X) or a new 12-digit NIC, our online ID checker decodes the mathematical structure to confirm authenticity and extract your registered birth date, gender, age, and day of the week.

What Details Can You Check From an ID Number?

📅 Date of Birth

The year and day-of-year code reveal the exact calendar date of birth, automatically accounting for leap years.

👤 Gender Determination

Female ID numbers have 500 added to the 3-digit day code (501–866), while male codes remain between 001–366.

🔄 Old & New Format Conversion

Instantly convert 9-digit old ID numbers to 12-digit new format, or reverse 12-digit numbers for pre-2000 births.

🗳️ Voter Status at Issue

Old NIC numbers display 'V' for registered electors and 'X' for non-electors or exemptions at time of issue.

Can You Check Sri Lanka ID Card Number by Name Online?

Official Privacy Law Clarification: You cannot search a person's name or home address from an NIC number online, nor search for an ID card number by full name.

Under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 and the Department for Registration of Persons Act, personal directories linking names and addresses to identity card numbers are confidential government records. Online tools like NIC Info perform algorithmic structural verification in your browser without contacting government databases.

In the old 9-digit format, 'V' indicates that the holder was registered as an eligible voter at the time the ID was issued. 'X' designates a non-voter or someone with an exemption. The new 12-digit format has no letters and uses a numeric check digit instead.

The 3 digits following the birth year represent the day of the year (DOY). For males, this is days 1–366. For females, 500 is added (days 501–866). Our tool takes this day count and converts it into the exact calendar month and date, adjusting for leap years.
