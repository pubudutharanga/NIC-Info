How We Calculate Exact Age From an ID Card

Your National Identity Card number contains your exact birth day code set by the Department for Registration of Persons (DRP). By parsing this embedded metadata and comparing it against the active calendar date, our algorithm computes your exact age in years, months, and days without external data lookups.

1

Birth Year Parsing. For 9-digit old NICs, the first 2 digits represent the birth year in the 1900s (e.g. 92 = 1992). For 12-digit new NICs, all four digits of the century are explicitly encoded (e.g. 1992 or 2004).

2

Day of Year Translation. Digits 3–5 (old format) or 5–7 (new format) encode the day of the year (001–366). Female codes have 500 added (501–866). The engine subtracts 500 when applicable and converts the ordinal number into calendar month and date.

3

Exact Calendar Delta & Countdown. Today's date is subtracted from the birth date adjusting for leap year rules and varying month lengths. The engine also computes the total days lived and the remaining countdown to your upcoming birthday.
